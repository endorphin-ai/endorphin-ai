# Endorphin Trademark Policy

Last Updated: Jan 21, 2025

### Introduction

This document outlines the policy for the use of the Endorphin™ name and any
associated logos (collectively, the "Endorphin Trademarks"). Endorphin is an
open-source project, and we welcome and encourage the community to use the
Endorphin name and logos for discussion, development, and community-building.

The goal of this policy is to ensure that the Endorphin trademark remains a
reliable indicator of quality and official source, while allowing the community
to thrive. This policy aims to clarify what is considered acceptable use of the
Endorphin trademarks, both for commercial and non-commercial purposes.

The Endorphin project is developed and maintained by Redstudio Agency.

### The Endorphin Trademark

The name "Endorphin" and the official Endorphin logos are trademarks of
Redstudio Agency. We have created these trademarks to protect the integrity of
the project and to provide the community with a clear way to identify the
official software.

### Permitted Uses (Without Asking Permission)

We want to make it easy for anyone to use the Endorphin name for
community-oriented, non-commercial purposes. You are permitted to use the
Endorphin name in the following ways without a separate agreement from us:

1.  **Referring to the Project:** You may use the Endorphin name to refer to the
    Endorphin project itself.
    - _Example:_ "I'm using the Endorphin framework to build my new testing
      suite."

2.  **Describing Compatibility:** You may use the Endorphin name to truthfully
    state that your software is compatible with or designed to work with the
    Endorphin framework.
    - _Example:_ "This plugin is compatible with Endorphin v1.2."
    - _Example:_ "Our platform now supports importing test results from
      Endorphin."

3.  **Community Advocacy:** You can use the Endorphin trademarks in
    non-commercial materials for community advocacy, such as in blog posts,
    tutorials, presentations, or videos.
    - _Example:_ A YouTube tutorial titled "Getting Started with Endorphin."
    - _Example:_ A blog post titled "Why We Chose Endorphin for AI Testing."

4.  **Forks and Modifications (Naming):** You are free to fork, modify, and
    redistribute the Endorphin source code under the terms of the AGPLv3
    license. However, you must use your own branding for your modified version.
    You may state that your software is a "fork of Endorphin" or is "based on
    the Endorphin source code," but you cannot use the Endorphin name as part of
    your project's name.
    - _Correct:_ "QuantumTest (a fork of Endorphin)"
    - _Incorrect:_ "Endorphin QuantumTest" or "Endorphin Pro"

### Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use the Endorphin trademarks in any of the following ways
without our explicit prior written permission:

1.  **In Project or Product Names:** You may not use "Endorphin" or any
    phonetically similar name as part of the name for your own software,
    product, or service.

2.  **Implying Endorsement:** You may not use the Endorphin trademarks in any
    way that implies an official affiliation, partnership, sponsorship, or
    endorsement by Redstudio Agency or the official Endorphin project.

3.  **Commercial Use on Products:** You may not use the Endorphin trademarks on
    any commercial product or for-profit service that is not the official
    Endorphin project. This includes using the name in advertising or marketing
    materials for your own commercial software. If you are interested in a
    commercial partnership, please contact us.

4.  **In Company or Domain Names:** You may not use "Endorphin" in the name of
    your company, business, or in any top-level domain name (e.g.,
    `www.endorphin-hosting.com`).

5.  **On Merchandise:** You may not use the Endorphin name or logos on any
    physical merchandise for sale, such as t-shirts, mugs, or stickers.

### Using the Endorphin Logo

The official Endorphin logo must always be used in its original, unmodified
form. Do not alter the color, proportions, or add any other elements to the
logo.
